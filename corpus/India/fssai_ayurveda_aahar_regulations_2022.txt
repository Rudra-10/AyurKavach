# FSSAI Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahar) Regulations, 2022

## Section 2(1)(a)
"Ayurveda Aahar" means food prepared in accordance with the recipes or books or principles specified in the authoritative books of Ayurveda listed in Schedule A of these regulations, but does not include Ayurvedic drugs or proprietary medicines under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, or items containing synthetic vitamins, minerals or isolated chemicals.

## Section 3(1)
Prior Approval and Regulatory Posture:
No person shall manufacture or sell Ayurveda Aahar products without prior approval or licensing from the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI). Products must carry the distinct Ayurveda Aahar logo and mandatory warning that they are dietary supplements and not intended to treat, cure or mitigate specific diseases.

## Section 4
Intellectual Property and Benefit Sharing:
Since Ayurveda Aahar formulations rely upon traditional dietary principles and recipes in ancient Ayurvedic texts, pure classical recipes are non-patentable under Section 3(p) of the Patents Act, 1970. Accessing raw biological ingredients from India for commercial manufacturing triggers compliance under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

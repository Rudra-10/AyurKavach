# Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023

## Section 3(2) (Amended)
Exemption for AYUSH Practitioners and Cultivators:
The 2023 Amendment explicitly exempts registered AYUSH practitioners (vaids, hakims), local communities, and traditional healers from requiring prior intimation to State Biodiversity Boards when accessing biological resources for treating patients or traditional healthcare.

## Section 7 (Amended)
Access and Benefit Sharing on Cultivated Medicinal Plants:
Indian entities accessing cultivated medicinal plants and their products are exempted from seeking prior intimation to State Biodiversity Boards, reducing compliance friction for commercial manufacturers of Ayurvedic medicines who procure raw herbs from certified cultivators.

## Section 40 (Amended)
Decriminalization and Civil Penalties:
The 2023 Amendment decriminalized offenses under the Act, replacing imprisonment with civil monetary penalties adjudicated by designated adjudicating officers.
